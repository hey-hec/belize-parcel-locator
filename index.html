Belize Parcel Locator

Upload a photo of a cadastral survey plan. If its grid coordinates (the pair of numbers ending in N and E, usually beside blue reference lines) are visible, this reads them and drops a pin on the map.
Uses Belize's standard metric survey grid (UTM Zone 16N). The entire country fits within this single zone — no configuration needed.
Tap to choose a photo
JPG or PNG · grid-coordinate labels (N and E) must be visible
Coordinate reading is done by an AI model — it can occasionally misread numbers that are blurry, cropped, or at an unusual angle. Assumes UTM Zone 16N (WGS84). If the result falls outside Belize the tool will warn you, which usually means the plan uses a different grid system.
